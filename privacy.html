iShallPass

Privacy policy

Effective from 1 October 2026

iShallPass does not collect any personal data for the developer. Your iSmartGate login stays in your car and is sent only to your own iSmartGate, so the app can open and close your garage door.

Who is responsible

iShallPass is developed and owned by Atstern AS, organisation number 837 920 892, Skansejordet 3b, 1383 Asker, Norway. Contact: webmaster@atstern.no.

iShallPass is an app for Android Automotive OS (for example Polestar 4). It shows the status of your garage door and lets you open and close it through your iSmartGate.

All communication with iSmartGate uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The app talks only to your own iSmartGate. iSmartGate's handling of your data is covered by iSmartGate's own privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • It does not send any data to Atstern AS or to any server run by Atstern AS.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • It does not use your location.
  • It does not share or sell data to anyone.
  • It does not back up your login to the cloud.

Google Play may collect data about installs and app usage under Google's own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet: to communicate with your iSmartGate.
  • Notifications: to tell you if the garage door has been left open. You can turn this off in the app or in the car's settings.

Deleting your data

All data is stored only in the car. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in the car's settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: webmaster@atstern.no.
