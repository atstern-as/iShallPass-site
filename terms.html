This is especially important because the app lets you close the door when you cannot see it.

Remote operation and access

Unlike a normal remote control, iShallPass works over the internet. The door can be opened and closed from anywhere the car has a connection, also when you are far away from the garage and cannot see the door.

  • Only open the door when you are present, or when you are sure that it is safe and that you have control over who gets access to the garage.
  • If the door is opened by mistake, or while you are away, you are responsible for closing it and for securing the garage. Always check the status in the app, or check the door yourself.

Your iSmartGate login is stored encrypted in the car. Anyone who can use your driver profile in the car can open and close your garage door with the app. Protect your driver profile, for example with a PIN code if your car offers it, and remove your login from the app (or uninstall it) before you sell or lend out the car.
